📶 Router Manager

Manage Wi-Fi, connected devices and settings on Huawei ONT fibre routers, right from your Android phone.

Get it on Google Play

✨ What It Does

  • Wi-Fi: change your network name and password, and manage extra and guest networks.
  • Connected devices: see who is on your Wi-Fi, block devices, and get alerts when a new device joins.
  • Internet control: turn internet access on or off for individual devices, and set parental controls.
  • Network tools: WAN status, DHCP and static IP reservations, data usage, speed test and router reboot.
  • Other routers: open any router's own admin page inside the app.

Everything happens between your phone and your router, over your own network. There is no account and no server: your router password and settings stay on your device.
